CalTrack

CalTrack is a simple calorie tracking app designed to help you keep track of your daily calorie intake, manage your food and drinks, and look back at your eating history.

Everything is presented through a clean dark glassmorphism interface with an interactive calorie progress circle.

- Daily Calorie Tracking
  Track your daily calorie intake and see how much of your calorie goal remains.

- Interactive Calorie Circle
  Visualize your calorie progress with the animated orange calorie circle.

- Food & Drink Logging
  Add foods and beverages with their calorie values to your daily intake.

- Food Photos
  Attach a photo when logging food or drinks.

- Saved Foods
  Save frequently consumed foods and drinks for quick access when logging calories.

- Quick Add
  Quickly add saved foods to your daily calorie intake.

- Calorie History
  Browse previous days and see what you consumed.

- Food Photos in History
  View the photos attached to your previous food entries.

- Daily Calorie Goal
  Set your personal daily calorie target and monitor your progress.

- Over-Limit Warning
  Get a clear warning when your daily calorie intake goes over your target.

- Dark Glassmorphism UI
  A dark, modern interface with glass-style elements and orange accents.

- Offline-First
  Core tracking features work without requiring an internet connection.

- Settings
  Manage your profile, calorie goal, and application data.
